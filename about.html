Our Story

About PrepVida

PrepVida.in was built with one simple belief: every student preparing for a job interview deserves a realistic, honest practice run — not just generic tips, but a genuine simulation of what their dream company's interview actually feels like.

We built PrepVida because most interview prep tools out there are either too generic (the same questions for every company and role) or too expensive for Indian students and job seekers. PrepVida is built specifically for India — priced in rupees, tuned to how Indian candidates actually prepare, and designed to feel like practicing with a real recruiter rather than a chatbot.

What we do

PrepVida uses AI voice technology to conduct realistic mock interviews tailored to any company and role a student chooses — from Google to a local startup, from Product Manager to Data Analyst. Every interview adapts its structure, tone, and questions to match how that type of role is genuinely interviewed, and can even reference a candidate's own resume for more personalized questions.

Our mission

To make high-quality, personalized interview practice accessible and affordable for every student in India — regardless of which college they attend or which company they're aiming for.
