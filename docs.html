Getting Started

1. Create your account

Sign up with your email and set a password. You'll receive a confirmation email — click it to activate your account.

2. Choose a plan

Visit Pricing and pick Basic, Pro, or Premium depending on whether you want voice-only or video interview practice.

3. Set your dream company & role

Tell us which company and role you're preparing for — every interview question is generated around this choice.

4. Take your interview

Start your session and speak naturally with the AI interviewer, just like a real round.

5. Check your inbox

Your scoreboard and feedback are emailed to you automatically once the interview ends. You can also view your full history on your Dashboard.
